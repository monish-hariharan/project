Statistics by month

Verdict uses the t-test at 5%: "Bullish"/"Bearish" when significant, otherwise "Leans" in the direction of the mean. Wilcoxon tests the median, the sign test tests the win rate against 50%, and "vs other months" (Welch t-test) asks whether the month beats the market's normal drift. Click a header to sort.

Method

Data: Dhan API v2 daily candles for the NIFTY 50 spot index, aggregated into calendar months. Dhan's history starts in January 2006. Monthly return is this month's close over last month's close; January 2006 uses its own open. No news, events or announcements are filtered or adjusted for: every month counts equally. The month in progress is shown above but left out of every statistic. With roughly 20 years per month, a single crash or rally year moves an average a lot, so read the median, win rate and Wilcoxon p-value alongside the mean.
