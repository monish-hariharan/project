Limitations of these recommendations

  1. Today's recommendation failed the risk gate (not the strategy this regime calls for) and is shown only because the gate is set to advisory. Treat it as the least-bad candidate, not as a trade with a measured edge.
  2. Short history. The range model, realised-vol forecast and drawdown inputs rest on 62 daily sessions (the Dhan connector returns history 5 candles at a time). Coverage and VRP estimates (61 sessions) have wide error bars, and 13 weeks cover one market regime.
  3. No historical option data, so no strategy backtest. Expected value, drawdown and stop probabilities come from a simulation, not from how these trades actually performed. The prediction log builds a real out-of-sample record from now on.
  4. Model, not market, distribution. Simulations use a driftless lognormal walk at the forecast realised vol (14.0%) with constant IV. Real NIFTY returns have fat tails, overnight gaps and volatility that rises when the market falls, so true drawdowns and stop-outs are likely larger than shown, especially for short-premium trades.
  5. Drawdown is peak-to-trough. Expected max drawdown counts open profit given back, so it can exceed the trade's maximum loss; it is a simulated average, not a cap.
  6. IV held constant. Mark-to-market assumes each leg keeps today's IV. A volatility spike widens losses on short options (iron condors) and helps long options, even if spot does not move.
  7. Prices are last trades, not quotes. The chain gives LTP and OI only, no bid/ask. Fills can differ; slippage is an estimate (0.5% of premium or 1 tick per order, more for thin strikes), not measured.
  8. Stops are checked hourly at best. Monitoring runs once an hour in market hours, and a stop price does not guarantee the exit price. Gaps and fast markets can exit well beyond the stop. Keep broker-side stop orders.
  9. GEX sign is an assumption. Open interest does not show who is long or short; the gamma flip and GEX regime use a calls-positive / puts-negative convention and have not been tested for predictive value.
  10. Fixed thresholds. Rich/cheap ratios, regime cut-offs, short deltas, wing widths and exit rules in rules.json are reasonable defaults, not optimised or validated values.
  11. Not modelled: margin requirements and margin calls, events (RBI policy, budget, results, global shocks) unless listed in event_dates, early assignment, exercise settlement details beyond STT, leg risk when orders fill one at a time, and taxes on profits.
  12. Position size uses a placeholder budget (capital × risk % in rules.json) until you set your own.
  13. Exit dates skip weekends but not NSE holidays. Check an exit date that falls on a holiday.
  14. These are model outputs for research and education, not investment advice.
