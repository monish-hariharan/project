Limitations of these recommendations

  1. Today's recommendation failed the risk gate (not the strategy this regime calls for; historical expectancy ₹-577 ≤ 0 (backtest 2019-02 to 2026-10, monthly expiries only (NSE bhavcopy, daily closes)); max loss ₹10,960/lot exceeds the ₹10,000 risk budget) and is shown only because the gate is set to advisory. Treat it as the least-bad candidate, not as a trade with a measured edge.
  2. Short history. The range model, realised-vol forecast and drawdown inputs rest on 63 daily sessions (the Dhan connector returns history 5 candles at a time). Coverage and VRP estimates (62 sessions) have wide error bars, and 13 weeks cover one market regime.
  3. Track records are history, not forecasts. Win rates, expectancy and drawdowns come from past trades; the next period can differ, especially across regimes the history does not cover.
  4. Forward record uses paper entries. Logged suggestions are marked at real last-traded prices from the time they were suggested; your actual fills, and checks only at run times (morning and hourly), make real results differ.
  5. Payoff ratio ignores probability. Max profit ÷ max loss after costs says nothing about how likely each outcome is; a condor with a good ratio can still lose more often than it wins.
  6. Prices are last trades, not quotes. The chain gives LTP and OI only, no bid/ask. Fills can differ; slippage is an estimate (0.5% of premium or 1 tick per order, more for thin strikes), not measured.
  7. Stops are checked hourly at best. Monitoring runs once an hour in market hours, and a stop price does not guarantee the exit price. Gaps and fast markets can exit well beyond the stop. Keep broker-side stop orders.
  8. GEX sign is an assumption. Open interest does not show who is long or short; the gamma flip and GEX regime use a calls-positive / puts-negative convention and have not been tested for predictive value.
  9. Fixed thresholds. Rich/cheap ratios, regime cut-offs, short deltas, wing widths and exit rules in rules.json are reasonable defaults, not optimised or validated values.
  10. Not modelled: margin requirements and margin calls, events (RBI policy, budget, results, global shocks) unless listed in event_dates, early assignment, exercise settlement details beyond STT, leg risk when orders fill one at a time, and taxes on profits.
  11. Position size uses a placeholder budget (capital × risk % in rules.json) until you set your own.
  12. Exit dates skip weekends but not NSE holidays. Check an exit date that falls on a holiday.
  13. These are model outputs for research and education, not investment advice.
